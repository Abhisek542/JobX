What it's like to work at Elastic

Here are a few things to know about us.

  • We're open (source)

    Elastic is built on a foundation of open source code, so openness and transparency are a huge part of our products — and our culture.

    We encourage collaboration across teams and timezones.

    What it means to be open source

  • We're distributed by design

    With employees working remotely in over 40 countries as well as on-site in 25 global offices, we bring together innovators from across the world, both virtually and in person. A distributed Elastic makes for a diverse Elastic, which makes for a better Elastic.

    What being distributed means to us

  • We solve real-world problems

    Over 50% of the Fortune 500 rely on Elastic, which means that we work with industry leaders in nearly every sector.
