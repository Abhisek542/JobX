About Sage Veterinary Imaging

Sage Veterinary Imaging operates North America’s premier outpatient veterinary centers for cross-sectional imaging and neuroimaging. Our mission is to improve the lives of pets by getting answers through advanced, human-quality diagnostic imaging.

Using the most advanced technologies—including 3T MRI, 128-slice CT, ultrasound, radiography, nuclear medicine, and digital cytology—we provide definitive diagnostic information for pets, their families, and referring veterinarians. In partnership with board-certified radiologists, neurologists, and other specialists, we help referring veterinarians make informed decisions and develop effective treatment plans.

Description

Radiologist – Remote

Sage Teleradiology, the tele-specialty services division of Sage Veterinary Imaging, is seeking board-certified veterinary radiologists to join our growing team.

In this role, you'll remotely interpret radiographs, ultrasound, CT, and MRI studies while collaborating with referring veterinarians to provide timely, high-quality diagnostic guidance. Beyond delivering accurate reports, you'll serve as a trusted clinical partner—helping veterinarians navigate complex cases, improve patient care, and make confident clinical decisions.

About Sage Veterinary Imaging

Sage Veterinary Imaging is one of North America's premier specialty diagnostic imaging organizations, bringing together advanced technology, collaborative medicine, and some of the world's premier cross-sectional imaging and neurologic imaging specialists. Through Sage Teleradiology, we extend that expertise to veterinary hospitals around the globe, providing collaborative, subspecialty-focused imaging support that helps clinicians deliver exceptional care to their patients.

Key Responsibilities

  • Interpret and report radiographs, ultrasound, CT, and MRI studies.
  • Validate the accuracy and clinical relevance of automated radiograph consultations.
  • Independently manage your caseload while collaborating with fellow radiologists on complex cases.
  • Consult with referring veterinarians regarding diagnostic findings, imaging recommendations, and case management.
  • Create Loom videos to guide referring veterinarians through complex or noteworthy imaging findings.
  • Contribute to quality assurance initiatives, workflow optimization, and continuous improvement efforts.
  • Stay current with advances in veterinary radiology, imaging technology, and evidence-based practice.

Time Commitment

  • Flexible scheduling ranging from as little as 4 hours per week up to full-time, based on demand and the radiologist’s availability 

Qualifications

  • Board-certified Veterinary Radiologist specializing in small animals (ACVR or EVDI)
  • Proficient in interpreting various modalities and providing clinical insights
  • Licensed to practice veterinary medicine in respective state
  • Organized with the ability to multi-task in a fast paced environment
  • Fluent English, excellent written and verbal communication
  • Strong communicator and collaborative team player
  • Passionate about advancing veterinary care through technology
  • Committed to continuous learning and professional ethics
  • The ability to work effectively remotely with minimal supervision

Benefits

  • Competitive compensation packages with full time, part time, and consultant positions available
  • Health insurance with medical, dental, and vision benefits
  • Continuing education time off and stipend
  • MRI fellowships, etc.
  • 401K retirement with employer match
  • Generous time away/off/PTO
  • Collegial work with colleagues in a non-competitive, supportive structure
  • 100% remote work
