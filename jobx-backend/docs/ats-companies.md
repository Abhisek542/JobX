## How to Discover More Companies

The resolution engine can discover **any company** on these 5 platforms:

1. **CATALOG** - Search the 22 pre-seeded companies
2. **URL** - Parse ATS links from careers pages
3. **SNIFF** - Fetch careers page HTML, find ATS links
4. **PROBE** - Derive tokens from company names/domains, test APIs
