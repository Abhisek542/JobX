Quick answers.

Which boards can it read?

Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable and SmartRecruiters. Anything else is clearly marked unsupported.

How fresh is fresh?

Boards are re-read every 30 minutes, and you can hit Check now whenever you don't want to wait.

Does it apply for me?

Never. JobX links you to the employer's own posting and you apply there yourself.

How long do roles stay?

Six days in your feed, then they clear out so what you see is always current.
